# Aura Dementia - concept and research pathway

> **Status: conceptual public research and design.** This is not a medical device, diagnosis, treatment, clinical advice or a substitute for professional care, assessment or emergency support.

Aura Dementia is a multi-page concept site exploring what a privacy-first digital companion for people living with dementia, families and caregivers might involve. It presents early ideas about memory, identity, connection, technology and a possible research pathway.

Visit the [public concept site](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/aura-dementia/) or inspect the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/auraofintelligence/aura-dementia).

## Read this as a concept

The site describes possible technology, family and caregiver experiences, research steps and future ambitions. It does not demonstrate a clinical product, establish a study, confirm approval or make health outcomes claims. Anyone seeking dementia support should use qualified health and care services appropriate to their situation.

## Find your way around

- [index.html](index.html) introduces the concept and the proposed support ecosystem.
- [technology.html](technology.html), [families.html](families.html) and [research.html](research.html) set out the conceptual technology, family and research-path material.
- [vision.html](vision.html) and [get-involved.html](get-involved.html) contain the broader vision and participation content.
- [LICENSE](LICENSE) sets the public-source terms for this repository.
